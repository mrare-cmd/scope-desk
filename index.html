Scope Desk is a research aid, not trading advice. It detects price differences; it cannot guarantee two markets resolve identically — that judgment is yours, which is why confirmation is manual. Prediction markets are largely efficient; genuine cross-venue arbitrage net of fees, spread, settlement timing and capital lockup is rare and often illusory. Kalshi trading requires US residency and KYC. The international Polymarket entity is not CFTC-regulated and US access may be restricted. Verify both rulebooks on the source sites before committing capital. Confirmation state is stored in your browser only.
