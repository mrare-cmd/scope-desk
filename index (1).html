Cross-venue prediction-market scanner. Surfaces price gaps between Polymarket and Kalshi, then forces a resolution-equivalence check before anything counts as a real edge. A gap is not an arb until the rulebooks agree.
How to read this: Pairs are matched on title + close date only — that is a weak signal, as the iPhone-18 case showed. Every match opens with both resolution texts side by side. A scope mismatch (different qualifying outcome, settlement source, or sub-event) means the gap is fake. Confirm equivalence once per pair; the choice is saved locally so you never re-check it. SCOPE·DESK is a research aid, not trading advice. It detects price differences; it does not and cannot guarantee two markets resolve identically — that judgment is yours, which is why confirmation is manual. Prediction markets are largely efficient; genuine cross-venue arbitrage net of fees, spread, settlement timing and capital lockup is rare and often illusory. Kalshi trading requires US residency and KYC. The international Polymarket entity is not CFTC-regulated and US access may be restricted. Verify both rulebooks on the source sites before committing capital. Resolution-confirmation state is stored in your browser only (localStorage).
